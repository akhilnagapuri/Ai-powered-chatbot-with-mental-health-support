TITTLE :-




AI POWERED CHATBOT &
SENTIMENT ANALYSIS FOR MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT

ABSTRACT :-

Mental health is just as important as physical health but not everyone has access to the support they need especially when they are feeling overwhelmed, anxious or down this project introduces an Ai-powered chatbot using natural language processing and sentiment analysis this chatbot can understand how someone is feeling just by reading their messages or tone. It responds with kind, supportive words and self-care tips .

This digital friend creates a safe and private space where users can express themselves without fear . It’s a simple but powerful way to make mental health support more available, personal and accessible when people need it most .
